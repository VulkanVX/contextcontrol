# ContextControl v0.4.2

Google research now handles blocked or unavailable source pages and continues with other results.

- HTTP access errors are detected when navigation finishes instead of waiting for the page-reading timeout.
- Reddit-style block screens, sign-in requirements and verification screens are excluded from article evidence.
- The local model receives the failure reason and can choose alternative results. Research makes at most five distinct page attempts and one replacement-selection call.
- Failed pages remain labelled as unread. When no pages are readable, the final prompt explicitly requires disclosure that the answer relies on Google snippets.
- Single-number link choices from small models are now accepted alongside arrays.

This update respects site access restrictions. It does not bypass Reddit blocks or sign-in requirements.
